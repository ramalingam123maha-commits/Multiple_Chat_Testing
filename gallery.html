The creative minds behind the collection

Painter

Elena Vasquez

Barcelona, Spain · Est. 2015

Elena's work is a conversation between colour and emotion. Trained at the Barcelona Fine Arts Academy, she blends classical oil techniques with contemporary abstract expression — her canvases feel like memories you never quite had.

Photographer

Marcus Chen

Tokyo, Japan · Est. 2012

Marcus captures the invisible language of cities — the pause between neon and night, the geometry of loneliness. His dual practice in film and digital photography won him the 2021 Asia-Pacific Lens Award.

Sofia Larsen

Stockholm, Sweden · Est. 2018

Sofia navigates the frontier where algorithms meet intuition. Her generative and 3D works explore digital consciousness — featured in MoMA's 2023 New Media exhibition and collected by galleries across Europe.

Sculptor

James Okafor

Lagos, Nigeria · Est. 2009

James forges stories from steel, bronze, and marble. His monumental sculptures address themes of identity, migration, and resilience — permanent installations stand in New York, London, and Nairobi.
